# Privacy Policy

**Amplify** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No analytics or tracking
- No data sent to external servers
- All settings (volume, EQ, site preferences) are stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`
- No cookies
- No user accounts

The extension requires these permissions:
- **storage**: To save your volume/EQ preferences locally
- **contextMenus**: To add right-click volume controls
- **tabs**: To show which tabs are playing audio

Source code: https://github.com/harisathwik/amplify
